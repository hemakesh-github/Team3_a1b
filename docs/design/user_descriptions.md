# Main users of StaySpot

The application's main users are Guests and Admin/Platform Staff.

## Guests

- Search for vacation rental properties, including location-based searches.
- View property details such as price, amenities, house rules, accessibility features, and reviews.
- Make bookings for properties.
- Use their wallet balance to pay for bookings.
- View their booking status, such as `CONFIRMED`, `CHECKED_IN`, or `COMPLETED`.
- Check in to a booked property.
- Leave reviews, ratings, and location-related tags after their stay.
- View their wallet balance and transaction history.

## Admin/Platform Staff

- Add and maintain property listings and property information.
- Manage amenities, house rules, and accessibility information for properties.
- Monitor guest bookings and booking statuses.
- Monitor guest wallet transactions and audit logs.
- Handle booking or payment-related issues when necessary.
- View property performance, such as total nights booked and gross revenue.
- Analyze booking trends using revenue analytics and rankings.
- Analyze guest search activity to identify popular search locations or hotspots.
- Analyze reviews, including rating distributions, common review tags, and average property ratings.
